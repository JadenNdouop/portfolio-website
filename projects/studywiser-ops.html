Product walkthrough — StudyWiser Business Operations Platform

The Problem

Students have access to information but lack tools built around how memory and retention actually work — leading to ineffective study habits and poor outcomes.

My Approach

Applied spaced repetition, cognitive load theory, and behavioral design principles to build a platform that adapts to each student's learning patterns using real usage data.

The Outcome

A fully operational EdTech platform — from the student product to the internal business infrastructure — running as the core of StudyWiser.

About this project

StudyWiser started as a question: why is it so hard to make learning stick? After studying both psychology and data science, I had the tools to answer it — and the motivation to build something about it.

This platform is the operational backbone of StudyWiser. It handles everything from student onboarding and learning path personalization to internal business tooling, analytics, and reporting. The student-facing side is built around proven learning science: spaced repetition schedules, interleaving, and retrieval practice — not just content delivery.

As founder, I've been involved in every layer: product design, architecture decisions, data modeling, and the behavioral logic that drives how the app interacts with users.

Key features

Adaptive learning engine — Personalizes review schedules based on individual memory curves and performance data.

Business operations dashboard — Internal tooling for managing users, content, and platform analytics.

Behavioral nudge system — Lightweight interventions designed to maintain study habits without being intrusive.
