System walkthrough — ERP Business Management System

The Problem

Businesses often run on disconnected tools — spreadsheets, email threads, and siloed software — making it hard to see the full operational picture in one place.

My Approach

Designed and built a centralized ERP system with modules for resource management, financial tracking, and reporting — unified under a single dashboard.

The Outcome

A working ERP platform that consolidates key business operations into one interface, reducing manual overhead and improving decision visibility.

About this project

This project came from a recognition that small and mid-sized organizations often struggle to manage operations at scale without expensive enterprise software. I wanted to design something that brought real ERP functionality — resource planning, inventory, financials, and reporting — into a more accessible and purpose-built system.

The system is built with modularity in mind: each business function operates as its own module but shares data through a unified layer, giving decision-makers a real-time view of the whole organization.

Key modules

Resource management — Track people, assets, and capacity across teams and projects.

Financial reporting — Revenue, expenses, and budget tracking with exportable reports.

Operations dashboard — Central command view for business health at a glance.
