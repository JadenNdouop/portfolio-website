About me

The person
behind the work.

I didn't take the straight path to where I am — and I think that's exactly why StudyWiser exists. My journey through psychology, then data science, gave me two lenses most builders don't have: a deep understanding of human behavior, and the ability to model it.

I believe the best technology is invisible. It doesn't try to change how people behave — it works with the way people already think. That belief is baked into everything I build.

Outside of work, I'm curious about cognitive science, obsessed with why learning is so hard to get right, and always up for a conversation about any of it.
