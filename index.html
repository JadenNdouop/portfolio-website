Chapter 01 Psychology

The Mind

It started with one question: why do people do what they do? That question led me to study psychology at UMass Lowell — where I learned to observe, question, and understand human behavior at a fundamental level.

"The most powerful technology in the world is still the human brain. I wanted to understand it before I tried to build anything else."

I left with research methods, cognitive science, and behavioral frameworks — tools I still reach for every day when designing products or solving problems.

Chapter 02 Data Science

The Data

Understanding people is one thing. Being able to measure, model, and act on that understanding at scale is another. That's what brought me to data science at Boston University.

"Data tells you what happened. Psychology tells you why. You need both to build something that actually works."

Machine learning, statistical modeling, and pipelines — but more importantly, the discipline of asking better questions before writing a single line of code.

📊

Chapter 03 Founder

The Build

At some point I stopped asking "what should someone build here?" and started asking "why don't I just build it?"

Education has a data problem and a behavior problem. Students are handed information without the science of how to actually retain it. I saw an opportunity to apply everything — behavioral science, data, product thinking — and founded StudyWiser.

"StudyWiser isn't a study app. It's what happens when you take learning science seriously."
