About

The person behind the work

I'm Jaden — a psychologist turned data scientist turned founder. My path gave me two very different lenses: one for understanding people, one for making sense of data. Together, they shape everything I build.

That combination led me to start StudyWiser — applying behavioral science and data to fix how students learn. Not by making apps flashier, but by making them smarter.

I'm based in Massachusetts and deeply interested in the overlap between how humans think and how technology can serve that better.
