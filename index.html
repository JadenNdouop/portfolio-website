Chapter 01 Psychology

The Mind

It started with a simple fascination: why do people do what they do? That question led me to study psychology at UMass Lowell — where I learned how to observe, question, and understand human behavior at a fundamental level.

"The most powerful technology in the world is still the human brain. I wanted to understand it first."

I learned research methods, cognitive science, and behavioral theory — frameworks that I still reach for every single day when I'm deciding how to design a product, write a message, or solve a problem.

Chapter 02 Data Science

The Data

Understanding people is one thing. Being able to measure, model, and act on that understanding at scale is another. That's what brought me to data science at Boston University.

I learned machine learning, statistical modeling, and how to turn raw information into decisions. But more importantly, I learned how to ask the right questions — because the quality of your data analysis depends entirely on the quality of your thinking.

"Data tells you what happened. Psychology tells you why. You need both."
📊
M.S. in Data Science
Boston University
Graduate
Chapter 03 Founder

The Build

At some point, I stopped asking "what should someone build here?" and started asking "why don't I just build it?" That shift led to StudyWiser.

Education has a data problem and a behavior problem. Students are given information but not the tools to actually retain it. I saw an opportunity to apply everything I'd learned — behavioral science, data, and product thinking — to fix that.

"StudyWiser isn't a study app. It's what happens when you take learning science seriously."

As Founder & CEO, I oversee product, strategy, and vision. The goal is simple: help students learn smarter, not harder.
